### 2. Core Component: Interactive Card Deck Carousel (Coverflow Snap Effect)
- **Component Style**: Horizontal smooth snap carousel displaying a series of romantic/conversation theme cards.
- **Focus Dynamics (Framer Motion / Embla / Swiper)**:

- **Top Actions**: Interactive Heart icon at top-right with toggle state (favorite/like).
- **Bottom Typography**:
  - **Title**: Bold white text (`text-xl font-bold`, e.g., "Safe Place", "Ride or Die", "Open Book", "Good Trouble").
  - **Subtitle/Tagline**: Muted gray/white text (`text-sm text-gray-300`, e.g., "The kind of conversation that feels like home.").
