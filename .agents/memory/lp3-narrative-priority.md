description: How the LP3 narrative selector resolves an inconsistency between prose rules and mandatory expected cases.
---

When a low-discovery answer is paired with cellphone/routine conversation and the goal of renewed proximity, the LP3 result must be “A ROTINA ENGOLIU VOCÊS”, even if vulnerability is “Algumas coisas”.

**Why:** The briefing's mandatory cases 1 and 7 explicitly expect the routine narrative for that combination, while an earlier priority paragraph would otherwise select the waiting-conversation narrative.

**How to apply:** Preserve the explicit mandatory-case outcome when changing or extending the LP3 selector; keep “Sim” vulnerability and difficult-conversation goals on the waiting-conversation path.
